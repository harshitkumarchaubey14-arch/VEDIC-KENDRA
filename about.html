परिचय

Dr. Shivesh Kumar Ojha

  • Sanskrit Scholar
  • Vedic Teacher
  • Traditional Vedic Practitioner
  • Dedicated to the Preservation of Sanātana Dharma
  • Serving through Jyotiṣa, Vāstu and Vedic Rituals

Dr. Shivesh Kumar Ojha has devoted his life to the study and practice of the Vedic sciences, following an unbroken path of traditional learning since 2003. Rooted in the Kāṇva Śākhā of Śukla Yajurveda, his scholarship spans Jyotiṣa, Vāstu Śāstra, and the performance of Vedic rituals and Anuṣṭhānas in strict accordance with scriptural tradition.

Having earned his Ph.D. from the Veda Department of Sampurnanand Sanskrit Vishwavidyalaya, Varanasi, and a Diploma in Jyotiṣa & Vāstu from Banaras Hindu University, he combines rigorous academic training with the living wisdom of the guru-śiṣya paramparā. Today, he continues this tradition through teaching, consultation, and the conducting of sacred ceremonies for families and institutions alike.

विद्या ददाति विनयं विनयाद् याति पात्रताम्

Vidyā dadāti vinayaṃ, vinayād yāti pātratām

Our mission is to preserve and transmit the authentic knowledge of the Vedas — Jyotiṣa, Vāstu Śāstra, and Vedic ritual practice — exactly as passed down through the unbroken guru-śiṣya paramparā, making this timeless wisdom accessible to seekers with sincerity, humility, and scriptural accuracy.

सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः

Sarve bhavantu sukhinaḥ, sarve santu nirāmayāḥ

Our vision is a world where Sanātana Dharma continues to guide individuals and families towards clarity, harmony, and well-being — where the wisdom of the Vedas remains a living tradition, not a relic of the past, and every Anuṣṭhāna is performed with complete scriptural fidelity.
