प्रश्न

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

For astrology consultations, please bring your accurate birth date, time and place. For rituals, specific requirements will be shared in advance based on the ceremony.

How is consultation done?

Consultations can be done in person at our Varanasi centre, or remotely via video call, phone or WhatsApp.

Can online consultation be booked?

Yes, online consultations are available worldwide and follow the same scriptural accuracy as in-person sessions.

How long does it take?

Duration varies by service — astrology consultations typically take 45–60 minutes, while rituals and Homas can range from 1 hour to several days for larger ceremonies.

Do you perform rituals outside the city?

Yes, Dr. Shivesh Kumar Ojha travels for rituals and ceremonies outside Varanasi upon request; please contact us to discuss arrangements.
